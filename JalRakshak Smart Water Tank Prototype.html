Design Thinking Methodology Framework (5 Stages)

Complete 5-stage breakdown of how JalRakshak (Save Water) v0.0.1 solves residential water tank overflow.

Stage 01

Empathize

Understanding Resident Pain Points

  • Busy Morning Rush: Residents switch on the submersible pump and get busy cooking or getting ready, forgetting to turn it off.
  • Neighbour Friction: Water pours down from the terrace onto parked two-wheelers, laundry, and walkways, causing daily arguments.
  • Blind Operation: Climbing to the rooftop in summer or monsoon just to check the tank level is inconvenient and unsafe for elders.
"We only realize the tank is full when water starts cascading down the terrace pipe!" — Surveyed Resident
Stage 02

Define

Core Problem Statement & Metrics

Problem Statement: Unmonitored domestic motor pump operation in urban houses and apartments leads to 50 to 200 Litres of clean potable water wasted per overflow event.
  • A 1.5 HP pump discharges ~40–60 Litres/min; just 3 minutes of overflow wastes 150+ Litres.
  • Causes structural terrace seepage, damp walls, algae growth, and excess electricity bills.
Target: Zero manual intervention & 100% overflow elimination.
Stage 03

Ideate

Why Non-Contact Ultrasonic IoT?

  • Rejected Idea 1 (Metal Dip Wires): Corrode rapidly in hard borewell water due to electrolysis and fail within months.
  • Rejected Idea 2 (Loud Buzzer Only): Annoying to neighbours and useless if nobody is near the switchboard to turn off the pump.
  • Selected Solution: Non-contact JSN-SR04T Ultrasonic Sensor on tank lid + ESP32 30A Relay Cut-off + Web Dashboard.
Zero water contact = Zero corrosion in hard water.
Stage 04

Prototype

Interactive Software & IoT Architecture

  • Automated Cut-Off: Instantly trips the pump contactor at 95% volume with SMS/App alert.
  • Low-Cost BOM (~₹650): Uses affordable off-the-shelf NodeMCU ESP32, waterproof transducer, and 30A relay.
  • Colony Scale: If deployed across 50 houses in a layout, saves ~1.72 Lakh Litres of groundwater every month!
Supports UN SDG Goal 6 (Clean Water & Sanitation).
Stage 05

Test

User Testing & Calibration Feedback

  • 95% vs 100% Cut-Off Test: Verified that stopping at 95% prevents inlet pipe surge overflow while maximizing usable storage.
  • Turbulence & Foam Calibration: Added 5-sample median filtering in firmware so splashing inflow water doesn't cause false readings.
  • Fail-Safe & Dry-Run Check: Tested manual override toggle and dry-run protection if inflow rate stays 0 L/min for 2 minutes.
Validated with users for 100% reliability & zero spill.
